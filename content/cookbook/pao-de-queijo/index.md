## Ingredients

- 500 g sour cassava starch (*polvilho azedo*)
- 250 ml milk
- 120 ml vegetable oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 200 g grated Minas cheese (or a mix of Parmesan and mozzarella)

## Steps

1. Bring the milk, oil and salt to a boil.
2. Pour it over the starch and mix until it forms crumbs. Let it cool.
3. Add the eggs one at a time, then the cheese, and knead until smooth.
4. Shape into balls with oiled hands.
5. Bake at 200 °C for 25 to 30 minutes, until golden.

## Notes

They freeze very well: freeze them raw on a tray, then bake straight from the freezer with a few extra minutes.
